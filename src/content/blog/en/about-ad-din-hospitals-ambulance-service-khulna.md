**For Ad-din Hospital's own ambulance in Khulna, call the hospital. Its number is on the hospital's official website.** We are a separate service, not part of Ad-din. We do not run its ambulances and cannot speak for it.

## If you need the hospital's own ambulance

- Call the hospital's emergency line from its official website.
- Give the pickup address and landmark, the patient's condition and a phone number that stays on.
- Ask for the fare and the arrival time before you agree.

## If you need a trip from Khulna to Dhaka

We take patients from a Khulna hospital or home to a Dhaka hospital, or to any other district. The usual route is by road through Jessore and over the Padma Bridge. See our [Khulna ambulance service](/ambulance-service-khulna/) for the route and fare.

1. Tell us the hospital the patient is in now, the ward and floor, and the destination in Dhaka.
2. Tell us what the doctor advised for the journey, and whether a family member will travel.
3. We confirm the vehicle and the full fare before we leave.

This is a long trip. Plan it with the treating doctor, and ask the Dhaka hospital to confirm it can receive the patient before you start. More on [Dhaka to any district](/inter-district-ambulance-service/).

## Ad-din in context

Ad-din is a well-known hospital network in Khulna. For services, departments, doctors and prices, use the hospital's own website. We do not publish them because they change.

Call **01919-339689** any time. A dispatcher answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
