**Yes, an ambulance crew can check the heart on the way: pulse, blood pressure, oxygen level and, if the ambulance has one, an ECG.** What they cannot do is give a final answer. The hospital confirms a heart attack with an ECG read by a doctor and blood tests.

If you think someone is having a heart attack now, stop reading and [call an ambulance](/call-an-ambulance-for-a-heart-attack/). This page explains what happens after the call.

## What the crew checks

1. **Symptoms and history.** What hurts, since when, medicines taken, past heart problems.
2. **Pulse, blood pressure and oxygen.** Quick readings, repeated on the way.
3. **ECG.** Small stickers on the chest and limbs record the heart's electrical signal for a few seconds.

## What the ECG can show

A heart attack often changes the pattern on the ECG. A trained crew can spot some of those changes and call the hospital so the team is ready when you arrive. This early warning can save time at the door.

## What the ECG cannot show

- **A normal ECG does not rule out a heart attack.** Some show no change at first.
- **Some ambulances have no ECG.** Basic vehicles may carry only a pulse oximeter and a blood pressure cuff. See [what ambulance crews carry](/essential-equipment-is-carried-by-ambulance-crews/).
- **Blood tests** that confirm heart muscle damage are normally done in hospital.

## What the crew does on the way

- Gives oxygen if needed
- Keeps the patient calm and comfortable
- Watches the heart rhythm
- Tells the hospital what they found

Medicines or other treatment depend on the crew's training and the doctor's orders. See [crew training](/what-is-required-for-ambulance-crew-members/).

## Which hospital?

The nearest hospital is not always the right one. A hospital with a cardiac unit can treat a blocked artery. If a doctor has already named a hospital, tell the dispatcher. For ICU-level transfers see our [ICU ambulance service](/icu-ambulance-service-dhaka/).

## What to tell the dispatcher

- Where the patient is, with a landmark
- Their age and what they feel
- Whether they have heart disease
- Which hospital, if a doctor has named one
