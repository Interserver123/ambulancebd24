**An ambulance carries equipment for five jobs: checking the patient, helping them breathe, stopping bleeding, moving them safely and keeping the hospital informed.** What is on board depends on the type of vehicle and the crew.

This is a general guide, not a list of our fleet. When you book, the dispatcher tells you what the ambulance for your patient carries. See our [ICU ambulance service](/icu-ambulance-service-dhaka/) for transfers that need more than a basic vehicle.

## 1. Checking the patient

- **Pulse oximeter.** A clip on the finger that shows blood oxygen and pulse.
- **Blood pressure monitor and thermometer.**
- **Glucometer.** A quick blood sugar check, important when someone is confused or unconscious.
- **ECG monitor.** Reads the heart's rhythm. More on [cardiac equipment](/what-equipment-is-available-in-cardiac-ambulances/).
- **Stethoscope.**

## 2. Breathing

- **Oxygen cylinder** with masks and tubing
- **Suction unit** to clear the airway
- **Bag-valve-mask** to breathe for a patient who cannot
- **Airway adjuncts** in several sizes

## 3. The heart

- **Defibrillator.** Delivers a shock for a dangerous rhythm. An automated version (AED) can be used with simple prompts.

## 4. Injury and bleeding

- **Dressings and bandages**
- **Tourniquet** for severe limb bleeding
- **Splints** for fractures
- **Cervical collar** for suspected neck injury

## 5. Moving the patient

- **Stretcher** that locks safely in the vehicle
- **Spine board or scoop stretcher** for injured patients
- **Straps and blankets**

## Basic ambulance and ICU ambulance

A basic ambulance carries the items above in simpler form. An ICU ambulance adds more monitoring, a ventilator or drip pumps, and a crew trained to run them. Doctors advise which a patient needs. Ask the treating doctor, then tell us. We match the vehicle.

## Children and newborns

Equipment must be sized for the patient: small masks, small collars, and for newborns a way to keep warm. See [NICU ambulance equipment](/nicu-equipped-ambulances-in-dhaka/).

## Equipment is not the whole answer

Equipment helps only if someone trained uses it. Ask who travels with the patient. Our [crew training guide](/what-is-required-for-ambulance-crew-members/) lists what to ask.
