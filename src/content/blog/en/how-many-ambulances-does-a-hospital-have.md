**There is no standard number. A hospital keeps as many ambulances as its size, work and budget allow, and many keep few or none.** That is why families often call an outside service.

## What decides the number

- **Size and beds.** Larger hospitals move more patients.
- **Speciality.** A heart or children's hospital may need specialised vehicles.
- **Budget.** Vehicles, crew, fuel and maintenance cost a lot to run all year.
- **Demand.** Hospitals in busy areas or with big emergency departments need more.
- **Location.** Hospitals far from other hospitals move patients further.

## Why many hospitals rely on outside services

- A vehicle sits idle much of the time and still costs money.
- Different patients need different vehicles. One hospital rarely owns them all.
- Peaks are hard to plan for. An outside service can add capacity.

## What this means for your family

Do not assume the hospital has an ambulance free. Ask the NICU or ward staff early: "Can you arrange the ambulance, or should we?" If not, call a service yourself.
