**Pick the ambulance by what the patient needs on the way.** The treating doctor can tell you. If you are not sure, the dispatcher helps you choose.

## The types

- **Basic life support (BLS).** Stretcher, oxygen, trained attendant. For stable patients who need monitoring and a safe ride.
- **Advanced life support (ALS).** Adds drips, medicines and heart monitoring, with a crew trained for them. For seriously unwell patients.
- **ICU ambulance (MICU).** A moving intensive care setup: ventilator, pumps, monitors. For patients who need ICU-level care between hospitals. See [ICU ambulance service](/icu-ambulance-service-dhaka/).
- **NICU ambulance.** For newborns, with warmth and breathing support. See [NICU ambulance service](/nicu-ambulance-service-dhaka/).
- **AC ambulance.** Air-conditioned. For emergencies, discharges and check-up trips. See [AC ambulance service](/ac-ambulance-service/).
- **Non-AC ambulance.** Same use, lower fare, no cooling.
- **Freezer van (lashbahi).** For taking a loved one home after death. See [freezer van service](/freezer-ambulance-service/).
- **Air ambulance.** For distances too far or hard by road. We arrange it through an aviation operator. See [air ambulance service](/air-ambulance-service-bangladesh/).

## How to match one to your patient

1. Ask the treating doctor what the patient needs on the way.
2. Tell the dispatcher in the doctor's words.
3. Ask who travels and what is on board.
4. Ask for the full fare before you agree.

A longer trip to another district may need a different vehicle from a trip across the city. See [Dhaka to any district](/inter-district-ambulance-service/).
