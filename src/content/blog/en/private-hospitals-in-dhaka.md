**Dhaka has many private hospitals. These eight are well known, listed by area so you can plan the journey.** We take patients to hospitals and we are not part of any of them. The list is not a ranking and not a medical recommendation. Ask the patient's doctor which hospital is right.

## Eight well-known private hospitals, by area

| Hospital | Area |
|---|---|
| [Square Hospital](/square-hospital-dhaka/) | West Panthapath |
| Evercare Hospital (earlier Apollo) | Bashundhara |
| United Hospital | Gulshan |
| LabAid Specialised Hospital | Dhanmondi |
| Anwer Khan Modern Hospital | Dhanmondi |
| Green Life Medical College Hospital | Green Road |
| Ibrahim Cardiac Hospital and Research Institute | Shahbag |
| BIRDEM | Shahbag |

Not every hospital takes every case. Specialities, beds and prices change. Check with the hospital.

## How to choose

1. **Follow the treating doctor.** If the doctor names a hospital, go there.
2. **Call ahead.** Ask the emergency desk to confirm they can receive the patient and who will meet you.
3. **Think about the road.** The nearest hospital is not always the quickest in traffic.
4. **Ask about the fare early** if a long transfer is needed.

## Getting a patient there

Tell the dispatcher the pickup, the hospital and what the doctor advised. We match the vehicle: a standard [AC ambulance](/ac-ambulance-service/) for most transfers, or an [ICU ambulance](/icu-ambulance-service-dhaka/) when a doctor advises it. We tell you the fare before we dispatch. For babies, see [NICU ambulance service](/nicu-ambulance-service-dhaka/).

## What we cannot tell you

We cannot say which hospital has a bed today, and we do not publish hospital prices or doctor lists. They change, and the hospital's own website is the right source.
