**The tools that matter most are simple: a phone line that is answered, a map, and a monitor on the patient.** Expensive extras help less than people expect.

## For finding the patient

- **Phone and WhatsApp dispatch.** The most important tool. A dispatcher who answers fast and asks the right questions.
- **Map apps and shared location.** Ask the caller to send a WhatsApp location pin. It beats a spoken address.
- **GPS on the vehicle.** Lets the dispatcher see where the ambulance is and give a real arrival time.

## For care on the way

- **Pulse oximeter and blood pressure monitor.** Cheap and in nearly every serious ambulance.
- **ECG monitor and defibrillator** for heart patients. See [cardiac ambulance equipment](/what-equipment-is-available-in-cardiac-ambulances/).
- **Ventilator and drip pumps** on ICU vehicles. See our [ICU ambulance service](/icu-ambulance-service-dhaka/).
- **Warming incubator** for newborns. See [NICU ambulance equipment](/nicu-equipped-ambulances-in-dhaka/).

## For the hospital end

- **Phone ahead.** A call from the crew lets the hospital prepare.
- **Written handover.** Papers and a clear summary of what happened on the way.

## Where gaps remain

- Dhaka traffic can beat any technology. Arrival times stay approximate.
- Equipment varies between providers, so ask what is on board.
- A device is only useful with a trained person to use it. See [crew training](/what-is-required-for-ambulance-crew-members/).

## What you can do

Send your location pin, keep your phone on and tell the dispatcher what the doctor advised. That helps more than any gadget.
